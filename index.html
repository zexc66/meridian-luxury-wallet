06 / THE ATELIER

One workshop. Two hands. No shortcuts.

Meridian began in a single room above a Marais saddlery, with one master, one apprentice, and a refusal to touch a sewing machine. Every wallet is cut, skived, stitched, and burnished at the same bench where the first prototype was made — because an object that claims to outlive its owner should be born in a place that can be visited.

07 / QUESTIONS

Before you acquire.

Each of the 250 units carries a permanent number and certificate. Choose an available number, add your monogram, and our concierge contacts you within 24 hours to arrange payment and hot-stamping. Your number is held for 72 hours during confirmation.

Worldwide, complimentary, and insured at full value. The wallet travels inside its raw linen dust bag, sealed inside the archival cedar box. Express delivery runs 2–4 business days from Paris; every shipment requires a signature.

Unworn units return within 30 days for a full refund — monogrammed pieces included, since your number simply returns to the pool. Return shipping is prepaid; the atelier covers duties on refunds within the EU and UK.

Nothing at all, at first — handling is the whole point. After the first year, a thin coat of cedar-nut balm twice a year keeps the grain supple; store it in its linen bag inside the cedar box when resting. Avoid radiators and rain-soaked pockets, and let it dry naturally if soaked.

Six cards plus folded notes, with the cavity precision-cut to hold them without stretching. The calfskin relaxes about 10% in the first month and then holds that shape for decades — the technical card above lists exact dimensions.

Your initials are hot-stamped by hand in 24k gold, sterling silver, or blind deboss before shipping — add three days to delivery. Monograms are struck once and cannot be changed, so we confirm spelling with you personally.
